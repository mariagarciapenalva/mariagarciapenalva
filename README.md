**MNHack8 · BSC, Barcelona · Apr 2026 – Oct 2026 · participant**

Fault-tolerance characterisation of silent data corruption from radiation-induced bit-flips (SEUs) in GPU-accelerated computing, extended to quantum computing as a second context. Built on the MNHack7 solver. Focus: unprotected registers, cache, shared memory and in-transit data (not covered by DRAM ECC), and behaviour at handoff points in hybrid pipelines, using large ensembles for statistically meaningful sensitivity results.

**MNHack7 · BSC, Barcelona · Sep 2025 – Oct 2025 · participant**

Assembly-free FEM solver for heat transfer in heterogeneous materials, accelerated with CUDA and MPI and ported to MareNostrum 5. Runtime went from ~5 h (unoptimised CPU baseline) to 1.8988 s after optimisation, GPU acceleration and porting. Presented to BSC mentors, MN5 staff and peers.
</details>
